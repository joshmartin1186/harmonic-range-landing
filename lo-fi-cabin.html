The pedal

What each control does.

Not a wall of presets. A few controls, each one a whole idea, meant to be pushed and lived with.

  • Cabin

    One fader for the warm, intimate, close sound of a small room. Everything moves together.

  • Storm tape space

    A tape echo that blooms in the gaps between phrases, a space-echo head pattern, a sleepy half-speed double, and a cassette-style stereo swim.

  • Hearth tape age

    How worn the tape is: wow, saturation, and an echo that loses a generation with each repeat.

  • Mix

    How much of the processed sound is blended with your dry signal.

  • Bypass

    Takes the effect out of the signal path, so you can hear what you started with.

  • EQ 7 bands

    Seven bands to tune the tone by hand, after the plugin has done its work.

  • Surge footswitch

    Your own phrases come back reversed, as swells that pan across the stereo field and ratchet up while it is on.

  • Tap

    Tap a tempo and every echo follows the beat, or follow the host's tempo instead.
